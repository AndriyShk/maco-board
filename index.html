How this is counted

  • Profit counts on the day it is locked in, the way XO's Net PnL does: when shares are sold, or when a resolved market pays out. Open positions don't count until then.
  • Each sale or payout is worth its shares × (price − your average entry price), from your public XO activity, the same figure the app shows. Fees are left out.
  • Markets and convictions are two separate boards; Pulse rounds have their own.
  • A day on this board runs from 00:00 to 24:00 UTC; today's numbers keep updating until it ends.
  • Everything on this page is today's, unless you pick another day in the leaderboard.
  • Private profiles aren't ranked. Make your profile public in XO settings to appear.
  • The Win rate tab needs at least 3 markets settled that day.
